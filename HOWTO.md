# StoryForge Kids v0.2 (Beta) – How to Use

1. **Open the app:** https://storyforge.leonlink.net (backup: https://storyforge-kids-beryl.vercel.app). Not linked from the main site.
2. **Build your hero:** type the child's name, pick girl/boy/kid, an animal friend, a favorite color and an adventure. Press Enter or tap *Start my story*.
3. **Read:** the hero's details are woven into every page. A page counter shows progress.
4. **Read to me:** tap 🔊 to hear the page aloud; each word lights up as it's spoken. Speed slider adjusts pace; ■ Stop ends narration.
5. **Make choices:** each choice changes the story. 2 adventures, 8 different paths, 5 endings.
6. **Start over:** ↺ quits the current story without counting it.
7. **Finish & print:** tap *Finish the story ★*, then 🖨 to print a keepsake of the story your child created.
8. **Reading tracker:** stories finished, words read, average grade level (Flesch-Kincaid), words per minute (includes thinking time). Saved only in this browser; *Reset tracker* clears it.

Notes: read-aloud uses the device's built-in voice (Chrome, Edge, Safari). No accounts, no ads, no data leaves the device.
