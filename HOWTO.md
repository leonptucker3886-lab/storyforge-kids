# StoryForge Kids v0.1 (Beta) – How to Use

1. **Open the app** at the subdomain (storyforge-kids.vercel.app; storyforge.leonlink.net once DNS is added). Not linked from the main site.
2. **Build your hero:** type the child's name, pick girl/boy/kid, an animal friend, a favorite color and an adventure.
3. **Start my story:** the first page appears with the hero's details woven in.
4. **Read to me:** tap 🔊 to hear the page read aloud; each word lights up as it's spoken. Use the Speed slider to slow down or speed up. ■ Stop ends narration.
5. **Make choices:** tap a choice button to change what happens next. Each adventure has multiple endings.
6. **Finish the story:** on the last page tap *Finish the story ★*.
7. **Print keepsake:** tap 🖨 to print a page with the full story your child created.
8. **Reading tracker:** shows stories finished, words read, average story grade level (Flesch-Kincaid) and average words per minute. Saved only in this browser; *Reset tracker* clears it.

Notes: read-aloud uses the browser's built-in voice (works in Chrome, Edge, Safari). No accounts, no data leaves the device.
